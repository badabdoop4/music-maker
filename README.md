Music Maker a tool made in vanilla Scratch that allows users to create, edit and share songs! 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Please note that this program is still in development, and will contain bugs. You can report issues on my Scratch profile or in the project's comments.

___

## Getting Started

> [!NOTE]
> For this tutorial, I'd recommend using **TurboWarp Desktop** to get the best out of Music Maker. 
> Open up the project in **TurboWarp** via the link in the Scratch project's description, and save the project as an `.sb3` file.
> Transfer the file to a safe location on your PC, then open up the project in **TurboWarp Desktop**.

Music Maker's main screen, or the **layout window**, is divided into four different sections:
- The **menu bar** is the rectangular dock at the top of the screen. By clicking on the different text labels, you can open **context** menus to manage your project, add new elements and open up configuration.
- The pill-shaped notch directly underneath the menu bar is the **control panel**. Here you can access the settings page, add **sound loops** and **sound effects** to your songs, and control media playback.
- On the top right corner of the screen is the **window switcher**. You can press the buttons to switch between the **layout**, **sound editor** and **MIDI editor** windows.

> [!TIP]
> If you are ever unsure about a button's function, you can hover over it. If using **TTS** mode (Text to speech), you can also translate the buttons into different languages by utilising Scratch's translation plugin ***(more info below)***

<br>

## Configuration

By clicking the **configuration** button in the **control panel**, you can access Music Maker's configuration window. From here you can modify your project, change the look and style of Music Maker, and configure accessibility settings.
The configuration window has three tabs, that you can select from the bar at the side of the window.
- **Project Settings**: Configure your project's tempo, time signature, and details.
- **Personalisation**: Change the look and feel of Music Maker.
- **Accessibility**: Configure text to speech and TTS language and modify or remove window animations.

> [!IMPORTANT]

## FAQ
Still got some questions? Take a look below or ask on my Scratch profile!

Q: Why is it so slow?\
A: Make sure you are running the project on TurboWarp.

Q: How do I save my project?\
A: Your changes are stored in lists, so they are automatically saved and embedded into the `.sb3` file when you save the project.

Q: How do I export my song?\
A: You can export your songs by clicking "File" in the menu bar, then "Export". Exported songs are optimised to run smoothly on Scratch.

Q: Will Music Maker receive updates in the future?\
A: Yes! Features such as the Sound Library and .midi file support will be added in the future, along with various bug fixes.
